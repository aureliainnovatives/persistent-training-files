# Cloud Operations Incident Severity Policy
## SEV-1
Widespread outage, critical transaction unavailable, active severe security impact, or major data-integrity risk. Immediate incident command and emergency procedure.
## SEV-2
Significant production degradation or elevated failures with material customer/business impact. SRE/CloudOps owns structured investigation and approved remediation.
## SEV-3
Limited degradation with low business impact and practical workaround.
## SEV-4
Informational/minor/non-production issue.
Severity is based on customer/business impact, not merely one infrastructure metric.
