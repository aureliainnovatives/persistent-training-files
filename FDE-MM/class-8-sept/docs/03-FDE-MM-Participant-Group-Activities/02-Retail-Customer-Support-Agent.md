## Customer Situation

A large online retailer wants an AI assistant to handle **order-status,
cancellation, refund, and product-support queries**.

-   Product FAQs and return policies are stored in documents.
-   Policies change frequently during campaigns.
-   Current order, payment, and delivery status comes from APIs.
-   Some refund requests can involve high-value orders.
-   Customer expects fast responses.
-   Support volume increases sharply during sales.

The customer says:

> **"Let's give the LLM our support documents and allow it to resolve
> every customer request automatically."**
